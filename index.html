I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Universitat Pompeu Fabra and the Barcelona School of Economics. I am a macroeconomist working on financial economics and monetary economics. My research studies how central-bank interventions in credit markets reshape competition, financing conditions and investment among the firms they reach.

I am on the job market in 2026–27 and will be available for interviews at the EJM and ASSA meetings.
